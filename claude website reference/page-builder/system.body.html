Design system

The rules both pages are built on, drawn from the Ctrl P brandbook and the concept boards. Version 0.7, here for sign-off before the rest of the site is designed.

Colour2 core, 6 accents

Colour

Black and white carry most of the page. The two tints are black at low strength, and the darker one matches the studio grey in the mockup photography so photos sit flush.

Accents arrive two ways: on physical things (tape, chips, stickers, a tile’s colour bar), and as one full section per page, the way the brandbook opens each chapter.

Type2 families

Type

Integral CF Extra Bold makes the statements. It is a capitals-only face, so it is never set smaller than 28px: headings, the hero, the category index, prices.

Neue Regrade does everything else, card titles included, always in sentence case. No small capitals, no tracked-out labels.

ComponentsFrom the packaging

Parts

The interface borrows from what a Ctrl P parcel looks like: tape, sticker tags, stamps, receipts, tickets.

Hover is one idea used everywhere: tape wipes across. The button, a category row, a print label, a filter chip. Nothing moves out of place, and the Products menu waits for a deliberate pointer move before it opens.

Stickers and Postman P

Stickers only sit on something physical: the tape, a parcel. Never loose on the page, and never on product cards. Postman P appears once per page, riding the tape or carrying the logo.

Motion and voiceKept quiet

Motion

Four movements, each tied to something physical. All of them switch off for visitors who ask for reduced motion.

  • Tape. Tape wipes in from the left: over a headline word, across a button or row on hover, across a filter or an option when it is picked.
  • Rise. The hero headline comes up line by line. 0.9s.
  • Settle. Content fades up 16px as it enters the screen. 0.6s.
  • Ride. Postman P cycles along the tape at the foot of the hero as the page scrolls. Nothing loops.

Voice

A friend who gets you: specific, a little cheeky, never salesy. Short enough to read at a glance.

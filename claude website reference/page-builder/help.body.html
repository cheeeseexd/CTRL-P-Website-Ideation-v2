Ordering

Is there a minimum order?{{I:plus}}

No. One poster is an order, and so is one sticker.

How do I order?{{I:plus}}

Pick a product, choose its size and options, then add your photo. You see it on the product before it goes in the cart.

Can I see it before I order?{{I:plus}}

Yes. You get a preview of your photo or art on the product before you pay.

Photos and art

What kind of file do I need?{{I:plus}}

A JPG or PNG straight from your phone is fine. The bigger the print, the sharper the photo needs to be.

Can I move or crop my photo?{{I:plus}}

Yes. After you add it, you can fill the print or show the whole photo, zoom in, turn it round and drag it into place.

Bulk and brands

Do you print in bulk?{{I:plus}}

Yes. The price per piece drops as the quantity goes up. For a large run, ask us for a quote.

Do brands still get no minimum?{{I:plus}}

Yes. Order one box to see it, then order the rest.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?{{I:plus}}

Anywhere in India. Whatever the pin code, we’ll post it.

How do I track my parcel?{{I:plus}}

Use the order number from your confirmation on the tracking page.
